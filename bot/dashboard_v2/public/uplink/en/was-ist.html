What is Uplink

One input, multiple destinations

Uplink receives your OBS stream and distributes it to your platforms in the right format. You set up access, platforms and your OBS docks in the existing dashboard.

Requested settings and current output

Turning a destination on means it should be included in your next stream. Only measured media activity shows whether Uplink is sending to it. A public confirmation that the platform is live is only shown when it is actually available.

Video and audio

Compatible media can be forwarded unchanged. Different video feeds or output profiles that are needed are processed on the server. Live and VOD mixes stay separate; the required video and audio sources must already be present in the input.

When something is missing

Rejected profiles, missing VOD audio or the need to renew platform permissions are shown at the affected destination. Saved settings alone do not prove quality or that a feature works.
