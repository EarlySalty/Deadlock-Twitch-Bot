Purposes and legal bases

We process personal data to provide login, subscription management, payment processing, dashboard features, platform connections, support and the secure operation of the service. The legal bases are in particular Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR for legal obligations and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for our legitimate interests in security, error analysis and abuse prevention.

Data we process

Depending on how you use the service, the following data in particular may be processed:

  • Twitch data: Twitch name, Twitch ID, OAuth status and profile data provided by Twitch.
  • Discord data: Discord ID, display name and role status, where required for community or admin features.
  • Google and YouTube data: YouTube channel ID, channel name, granted permissions, OAuth tokens and expiry times, and the YouTube data needed for connected upload and livestream features.
  • Subscription and billing data: plan, status, booking time, invoice references and tax-relevant information.
  • Usage and analytics data: stream statistics, viewer history, chat and dashboard metrics, where needed for features you have enabled.
  • Technical data: IP address, user agent, timestamps, log data, security events and session cookies.

Google API Services and YouTube

When you connect YouTube to the service, the application gets access to the permissions you approve in the Google consent screen. Publishing approved clips uses youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. The YouTube destination for Uplink livestreaming uses youtube.force-ssl.

The application uses Google user data for the YouTube feature you enabled. This includes detecting and displaying the connected channel, uploading approved videos, reading the channel and video metadata needed for the upload and, for Uplink, retrieving the livestream destination information provided by YouTube.

Depending on the feature, we store the YouTube channel ID and channel name, the granted permissions, token expiry times and OAuth access and refresh tokens. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted. For Uplink, the livestream destination information required for forwarding may also be processed.

Google user data is not sold, not used for personalised advertising and not used to train general-purpose AI models. It is not shared with other companies for their own purposes. Google and YouTube receive the API calls required for the requested feature. Technical service providers may process data as part of operating the service and on our instructions.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements described there.

You can disconnect YouTube in the respective dashboard and additionally revoke Google access under Google account permissions. You can request deletion of stored personal data at mail@earlysalty.com, unless statutory retention obligations prevent it.

Recipients and service providers

Payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. Stripe processes payment data under its own privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

For login and platform features, data is exchanged with Twitch, Discord, Google, YouTube and other platforms you connect, as far as this is required for the selected feature. Cloudflare Turnstile may be used to protect certain legal pages by detecting automated access.

Cookies

This website uses technically necessary cookies, in particular for login sessions, subscription management and protected legal pages. The application does not set marketing cookies. Consent is not required for strictly necessary cookies under Section 25(2) No. 2 of the German TDDDG. Stripe may set cookies on its own domains during checkout.

Retention

Data is stored for as long as it is needed for the purposes stated above. Subscription and usage data is generally stored for the duration of the contract. Platform connections and the related OAuth data are kept for operating the enabled connection. After you disconnect, they are no longer used for further platform actions; you can additionally request deletion. Invoice and booking data may be stored for up to 10 years due to statutory retention obligations. Security and server logs are deleted regularly unless longer retention is required to investigate abuse or incidents.

Your rights under Art. 15 to 22 GDPR

  • Access to stored data under Art. 15 GDPR
  • Rectification of inaccurate data under Art. 16 GDPR
  • Erasure of your data under Art. 17 GDPR
  • Restriction of processing under Art. 18 GDPR
  • Data portability under Art. 20 GDPR
  • Objection to processing under Art. 21 GDPR

To exercise these rights, contact mail@earlysalty.com.

Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority is the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz (LfDI), Hintere Bleiche 34, 55116 Mainz, Germany.
